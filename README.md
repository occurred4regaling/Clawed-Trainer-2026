## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Clawed**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for exploring Prehistoria, managing survival needs, solving objectives, and fighting dinosaurs. It is designed for the single-player experience and can reduce resource pressure without changing the game's core exploration and puzzle structure.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to explore Prehistoria with reduced survival pressure, practice combat against dinosaurs, revisit puzzles, or complete the single-player campaign at a more relaxed pace.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

- **Infinite Health** — Prevents the player character from losing health during encounters and environmental hazards.

### 📌 Survival

- **No Hunger** — Stops hunger from decreasing in Survival Mode.

### 🌍 World

- **Time Control** — Pauses or adjusts the passage of time during Survival Mode.
- **Weather Control** — Prevents unwanted effects from dynamic weather conditions in Survival Mode.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Use the trainer after loading into the game world. Infinite stamina is useful when traversing the park, underground tunnels, aquatic areas, and dinosaur enclosures. Disable movement-related assistance before cutscenes, scripted sequences, or loading transitions if unusual behavior occurs.

### Tools & Inventory

Inventory-related options are most useful in Survival Mode, where scavenged resources support continued exploration. Activate unlimited consumables or inventory protection only after the inventory has fully loaded, and avoid changing item states while opening containers or transferring equipment.

### World & Environment

Classic Mode presents the original survival horror experience, while Survival Mode adds hunger, thirst, dynamic weather, a complete day-and-night cycle, and random dinosaur encounters. Time and weather options are intended for Survival Mode and should be disabled before saving or changing areas.

### Combat & Weapons

Clawed combines firearms and other weapons with encounters against dangerous prehistoric creatures, including Deinosuchus and Spinosaurus. Activate health, ammunition, damage, or enemy-control options before engaging a target. Disable one-hit and freeze effects when solving combat-related objectives that require normal enemy behavior.
